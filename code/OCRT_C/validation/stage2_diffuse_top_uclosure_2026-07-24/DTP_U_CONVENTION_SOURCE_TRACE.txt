OCRT diffuse-top U-field convention trace (2026-07-24)

1. Atmospheric BOA field
   src/rt_solver.c:492-495 documents I/Q cosine and U sine reconstruction.
   src/rt_solver.c:863-893 copies the downward total_u Fourier coefficient
   directly into boa_export->U_per_m. No sign conversion is applied.

2. Air-to-water coupling
   src/rt_air_water_coupling.c:183-220 contracts the BOA field with the
   T_aw Fourier matrix as an ordinary 3x3 matrix-vector product and writes
   the resulting Uw directly to coupled->U_inwater_per_m.
   FIX3 has already folded the m>0 incoming-U storage sign into M13/M23.
   No downstream U sign conversion is applied.

3. Water external-top field
   src/rt_solver.c:3948-3967 copies coupled.U_inwater_per_m directly to
   ocrt_dt_U and assigns w_opts.ext_top_U = ocrt_dt_U.
   The same direct assignment is used in the diagnostic/outer-coupling paths
   at lines 5365 and 5458. No U sign conversion occurs between the interface
   contraction and the water boundary source.

4. Production water SOS convention
   src/rt_sos_operator.c:371-394 defines the signed-direction vector source
   operator. For a downward incident U basis, I/Q output terms carry the
   minus signs shown in lines 372-386, while the U output is wrapped by the
   global minus in lines 387-394.

5. Diffuse-top injector
   src/rt_water_rt.c:669-856 represents a discrete incident field by
   AI/AQ/AU = 2*w*X and ch_c = 0.5*exp(-tau/mu), which exactly reproduces the
   Gauss weight and attenuation used by the production operator.
   Because src_u has an existing outer minus, the molecular incoming-U
   coefficients ruI/ruQ/ruU must be negative to match the operator.

Conclusion
   ext_top_U is in the same sine-Fourier Stokes-U convention as the water SOS
   field. The three incoming-U molecular coefficients must be negated. This
   is an internal operator-closure requirement, not an OSOAA empirical fit.
